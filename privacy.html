Privacy Policy

CaseAgainst Studio · Last updated 31 October 2026

CaseAgainst Studio is a private desktop application used by a single person, the creator of The Case Against, to upload episodes to his own YouTube channel. It has no other users.

What the app accesses

When its owner connects his Google account, the app uses the YouTube Data API to upload videos, set thumbnails, add captions and add videos to playlists on that channel, and to read basic channel information needed to do so.

What the app stores

The Google sign-in token is stored only on the owner's own computer so that uploads keep working. No Google or YouTube data is sent to any other server, sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be withdrawn at any time from the Google account's security settings (Third-party connections), or by choosing Disconnect in the app, which deletes the stored token.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Use of YouTube features is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
